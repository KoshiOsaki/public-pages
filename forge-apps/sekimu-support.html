Before contacting support

Check the documentation and troubleshooting steps. Your request should include:

  • your Confluence site URL;
  • what you expected and what happened instead;
  • the approximate time of the issue and your time zone;
  • steps that reproduce the issue; and
  • a screenshot with confidential information removed, if helpful.

Do not send passwords, API tokens, payment information, or confidential page content.

Security reports

Report a suspected vulnerability privately to k.osaki@dokokichi.com with the subject “SEKIMU security report.” Include reproduction details and potential impact, but do not access data that is not yours or perform disruptive testing.

Privacy requests

For privacy questions or requests, use the same email address with the subject “SEKIMU privacy request.” Identify the relevant Confluence site and Atlassian account, but do not include confidential page content. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Supported environment

SEKIMU supports Atlassian Cloud Confluence through the Atlassian Marketplace. It does not support Confluence Data Center or Server.
