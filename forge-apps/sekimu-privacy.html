SEKIMU Privacy Policy

Effective: October 30, 2026 · Provider: Koshi Osaki

DocumentationSupport

Scope

This policy describes how SEKIMU, an Atlassian Forge app for Confluence, processes information. SEKIMU runs on Atlassian Forge and does not send customer data to infrastructure operated by the provider.

Information processed

SEKIMU processes only the information needed to provide page governance features:

  • Confluence page identifiers, space identifiers, page titles, and page version information;
  • Atlassian account identifiers for page owners, reviewers, approval requesters, and review performers;
  • review schedules, review and approval states, timestamps, reminder timestamps, and rejection comments; and
  • app settings selected by a site administrator.

SEKIMU does not collect passwords, payment-card data, or the body text of Confluence pages for storage.

Purpose and legal basis

Information is processed to provide the app features requested by the customer: assigning page ownership, scheduling reviews, recording approvals, finding stale pages, and posting reminders. The customer controls installation and use of the app and is responsible for determining the applicable legal basis for its users.

Hosting, sharing, and transfers

Data is stored in Atlassian-hosted Forge storage and is isolated by Atlassian installation. SEKIMU has no external data egress, external analytics, advertising trackers, or provider-operated database. Atlassian processes information as the infrastructure provider under the customer’s agreement with Atlassian. The provider does not sell personal information or share it for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Governance records are retained while the app is installed and the records are needed for its features. Records for deleted Confluence pages are removed by scheduled maintenance. Account identifiers reported by Atlassian as closed or requiring an update are removed from governance records. After app uninstallation, Atlassian retains Forge hosted-storage data for up to 28 days before deletion under Atlassian’s platform retention process.

Security

SEKIMU uses Confluence permissions when displaying or changing page information, limits administrative settings to site administrators, and uses Atlassian-hosted runtime and storage. The app requests only the scopes needed for its documented functions.

Your choices and requests

Site administrators can uninstall SEKIMU. For questions or requests concerning access, correction, or deletion, contact k.osaki@dokokichi.com. Please identify the Confluence site involved; do not email confidential page content.

Changes

Material changes will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date.
